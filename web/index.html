II. The practice

Care is a structure,
not a finish.

Let each piece stand on its own.
Let it also belong to something larger.

These are exploratory studies, not production integrations. The public edition is curated; private experiments and operational records are not a live feed into this page.

Continuity
Carry what matters. Let corrections travel with it.
Identity
Keep names, records and authority distinct.
Privacy
Disclose only what the encounter needs.
Relationship
Offer connection without making it an obligation.
Trust
Show what is known, and how it is known.
